# Catalog seed (M4)

YAML files: `features.yaml`, `providers.yaml`, `service_lines.yaml`, `plans.yaml` (with
`exclusion_group`, `entitlements`, `prerequisites`, `eligibility`) and `observations.yaml` (manual
price observations, each with official URL, copy date and a complete `PriceBinding`).
Prices must be copied from the official India pricing page on the day they are entered — never from
memory, news articles or comparison sites. Record every provider in `docs/data-sources.md`.
